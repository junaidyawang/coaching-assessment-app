Your Centroid Position

Horizontal axis measures focus (Past/Problem vs Future/Solution). Vertical axis measures approach (Tell/Advocate vs Ask/Inquire).

Top-Right: Coaching Ask + Future/Solution. Co-creates answer, fosters accountability.
Bottom-Right: Mentoring Tell + Future/Solution. Always provides the answer & wisdom.
Top-Left: Counseling Ask + Past/Problem. Deep dives into root causes & emotions.
Bottom-Left: Consulting Tell + Past/Problem. Evaluates situation & prescribes expert fix.
